Your information, your choice

The contact form creates a message in your browser. You review it before choosing to open WhatsApp; no enquiry is sent automatically.

01 / PRIVACY NOTICE

About this notice

This notice explains how the MZG Car Rental website handles information when you explore our Lahore rental options or choose to contact us. It describes the website as it currently works, including the links that open email, phone and WhatsApp services.

MZG Car Rental is your contact for enquiries made through this website. For privacy questions, email contact.zulqurnain@gmail.com or call +92 302 5184681.

02 / PRIVACY NOTICE

Information you choose to provide

The contact form allows you to enter your name, telephone number, optional email, enquiry type, preferred car, travel dates, pickup area and a message. These details are used to prepare an enquiry for your review.

Entering the form does not send it to MZG. Form values are held in the open page and are not intentionally saved in browser storage by this website. The booking estimate also works in your browser and does not create a reservation record.

If you choose to open WhatsApp from the review screen, your prepared message is passed to WhatsApp. You decide whether to send it. If you send an email, call or message MZG directly, we receive the information you share through that channel.

03 / PRIVACY NOTICE

How enquiry information is used

Information you send to MZG may be used to respond to your questions, prepare a rental quotation, discuss availability, arrange pickup or return, and handle rental-related changes or concerns. Please share only the information necessary for your enquiry.

Actual rental arrangements may require identification, licence verification or additional records. The contact form does not accept document uploads. Ask MZG about an appropriate verification method before sharing sensitive documents.

04 / PRIVACY NOTICE

Cookies, storage & website assets

The website does not set advertising cookies, run third-party analytics or use local storage to save your enquiries. If you create an MZG account, the server stores your name, email address, a salted password hash, account creation time and terms acceptance time. Plain-text passwords are not stored. Your profile may also contain a Pakistani mobile number, city, country and optional photo. Uploaded photos are decoded, resized and re-encoded without their original metadata. Rental requests, quotations, statuses, cancellation records and history are stored under your account and available only to you and authorised MZG administrators. Booking drafts may be kept in temporary browser session storage for up to 30 minutes to resume the form after sign-in; they are removed after submission and do not prove authentication. Car images and the DM Sans font are served from the website itself. Signing in sets an essential HttpOnly, SameSite session cookie. Sessions last up to seven days, with a two-hour idle limit; sign-out, password changes and password resets revoke sessions. The server stores hashed session tokens, short-lived anti-abuse counters and expiring, hashed verification/reset tokens in private SQLite storage. Email verification and password recovery use the configured email provider; if delivery is unavailable, the website reports the limitation.

Like other websites, the hosting environment may process technical connection information such as an IP address, requested page and browser information when serving a page. Browser or device features may also remember information independently of this website.

05 / PRIVACY NOTICE

WhatsApp, email & other external services

WhatsApp links open a service operated by a third party. Phone and email links open your chosen device application. These services handle information under their own terms and privacy notices. Review those notices before sharing personal information.

Information you voluntarily send to MZG may be handled by the email or messaging providers used for that conversation. Verification and password-reset emails use the SMTP provider configured by MZG, which receives the destination email address and the email contents. Image-credit links also lead to external websites, but those websites are not contacted just because the credit link appears on a page.

06 / PRIVACY NOTICE

Keeping information & sharing it

This website has no enquiry database. Messages you actually send remain in the communication channel used to contact MZG. Rental-related records may be needed to manage the arrangement, resolve an issue or meet applicable record-keeping obligations.

For questions about a particular message or record, contact MZG using the published email or phone number. We do not need your identification documents to answer an initial vehicle enquiry. Do not send passwords, payment card details or unrelated personal information.

07 / PRIVACY NOTICE

Your choices & requests

You can browse the fleet without completing the contact form. You can edit or discard your enquiry before opening WhatsApp and can close WhatsApp without sending it.

You may contact MZG to ask what information has been received, correct enquiry details, or request deletion of information you have shared. Explain which conversation or booking your request relates to. Any information that must be retained for an ongoing arrangement, a dispute or an applicable obligation will be discussed with you.

08 / PRIVACY NOTICE

Protecting your information

Avoid sharing sensitive documents through the general enquiry form or public messages. Confirm that you are communicating with the phone number or email published on this website before sending documents or arranging payment.

No communication method can guarantee complete security. If you think information was sent to the wrong recipient or you notice a privacy issue, contact MZG promptly.

09 / PRIVACY NOTICE

Updates & contact

For account access, correction or deletion requests, contact contact.zulqurnain@gmail.com. MZG will verify the request before changing account data. This notice should be updated when the website adds new data collection, payment, analytics or other features. The date below identifies this version.
