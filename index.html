GOOD TO KNOW

A few answers before you go.

Where can I rent a car?

This website is for MZG Car Rental in Lahore. You can select city pickup, airport pickup or delivery within Lahore.

Are these the final rental prices?

The displayed PKR rates are used to calculate your rental estimate. Your written quotation confirms actual availability, deposit, insurance and any additional charges.

Can I book weekly or monthly?

Yes, you can enquire about daily, weekly and monthly arrangements. Review a rental estimate in the car panel or contact MZG for a quotation tailored to your dates.

Is airport pickup available?

You can choose Lahore airport pickup as your preferred option. Pickup time, meeting point and any charges must be confirmed.

What is included in the rental?

Each rental includes 200 km per day, pooled across your trip. A category-based refundable deposit applies. Free cancellation is available at least 24 hours before pickup. Read our rental policy for full terms. Rental estimates do not include additional charges.

Does this website confirm a booking?

Choose a car to review an estimate, then send a rental enquiry to MZG on WhatsApp. Availability, final price and the rental agreement must be confirmed with the team. No payment is collected on this website.
