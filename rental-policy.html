01 / MZG RENTAL POLICY

Refundable security deposit

The self-drive security deposit is separate from the rental fee. The standard deposit depends on the vehicle category:

Car categoryRefundable depositExtra kilometre
EconomyPKR 20,000PKR 20 / km
SedanPKR 30,000PKR 25 / km
SUVPKR 50,000PKR 40 / km
PremiumPKR 100,000PKR 60 / km

A deposit receipt and a joint handover inspection are provided for the rental. The deposit covers documented outstanding charges such as fuel shortfall, excess mileage, traffic fines or damage for which the renter is responsible. It is not automatically a limit on liability.

The undisputed deposit balance is returned within 7 business days of return and inspection. If a documented charge or insurance claim remains unresolved, MZG explains the amount retained and returns the remainder. Any retained balance is settled after the matter is resolved. No unexplained or automatic deductions apply.

02 / MZG RENTAL POLICY

Mileage & fuel

Each paid rental day includes 200 km. The allowance is pooled across the confirmed rental period: a five-day rental includes 1,000 km. Extra kilometres are charged at the category rate in the table above. There is no refund for unused kilometres.

Fuel is not included in the rental fee. Return the vehicle at the same recorded fuel level as handover. Any shortfall is charged at the actual pump cost supported by a receipt. A different allowance for a corporate or long-term quotation must be agreed in writing before confirmation.

Pickup and return are arranged in Lahore. Outstation travel, off-road use and additional drivers require prior written approval; cross-border travel is not permitted.

03 / MZG RENTAL POLICY

Insurance & damage responsibility

Vehicle-specific insurance details must be disclosed before a rental is confirmed or payment is requested. The written quotation identifies the applicable insurer or cover arrangement, permitted drivers, exclusions, territorial limits and the renter's excess. MZG verifies that the intended rental use and driver are eligible under the applicable policy.

No blanket promise of comprehensive insurance, zero excess, personal accident cover or theft protection is made on this website. A refundable deposit is not an insurance premium. Optional protection is charged only if its cost and terms are explained and the renter chooses it.

Pre-existing damage is recorded at handover and is not charged to the renter. Responsibility for new damage is assessed from the inspection records, rental agreement and insurer's decision. Existing rights under applicable law remain unaffected.

If there is an accident, theft or breakdown, move to safety where possible, contact the appropriate emergency services when needed and notify MZG promptly. Follow the insurer's and authorities' reporting requirements. Do not authorise repairs on MZG's behalf without permission.

04 / MZG RENTAL POLICY

Cancellation, changes & refunds

Cancel at least 24 hours before the confirmed pickup time for a full refund of the prepaid rental charge. No cancellation charge applies to an unconfirmed enquiry.

For cancellation within 24 hours of pickup or a no-show, the charge is one daily rental fee, capped at the rental amount already prepaid. Remaining prepaid rental fees and any unused security deposit are refunded. There is no additional cancellation bill beyond that prepaid amount.

If MZG cannot provide the confirmed vehicle or an alternative you accept, the prepaid rental fee and deposit are refunded in full. Approved refunds are initiated within 7 business days; bank processing may take additional time.

Date changes and extensions depend on availability. Contact MZG before the agreed pickup or return time. No revised rate or fee applies until it is explained and accepted. Early returns do not automatically reduce the confirmed rental fee; any agreed adjustment is recorded in writing.

05 / MZG RENTAL POLICY

Rental days, pickup & late return

A rental day is 24 hours from the confirmed pickup time. Choose your dates and times in the booking form; a part-day is rounded up to a rental day in the estimate. The quotation confirms the exact schedule.

A 60-minute return grace period applies. After that, agreed late-return charges accrue at one twenty-fourth of the daily rate for each additional started hour, capped at one daily rate per extra 24-hour period. Contact MZG before you are late; an extension is subject to vehicle availability.

Airport parking, delivery, collection, waiting time and any optional driver service are separately quoted before confirmation. Fuel and traffic fines are not included. Applicable taxes, if any, are shown in the final quotation. There are no undisclosed extras.

06 / MZG RENTAL POLICY

Driver eligibility & documents

The standard self-drive minimum age is 21 years, with a valid driving licence held for at least one year. A vehicle's insurer may require a higher age or longer licence history; any stricter requirement is explained before confirmation.

Residents should have their original CNIC and valid driving licence ready for verification. Visitors should ask MZG to confirm the documents and driving permissions applicable to their stay. Do not upload CNIC, licence or payment card details to the public enquiry form.

Only approved drivers named in the agreement may drive the vehicle. Do not transfer, sub-rent, overload or use it for racing, illegal activity or an unapproved purpose. Keep a copy of the agreement, handover record and MZG contact details throughout the rental.

07 / MZG RENTAL POLICY

How a booking becomes confirmed

Choose a car and your preferred dates and times, review the rental estimate and submit your request on WhatsApp. MZG checks actual vehicle availability and replies with the full quotation, deposit amount, insurance information and pickup arrangements.

A website estimate or WhatsApp request is not an instant reservation. A booking is confirmed only when MZG issues a written confirmation and the required agreement and payment arrangements are completed. Do not pay an unverified recipient.
